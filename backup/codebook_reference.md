You will read a comment from the Reddit forum r/AmItheAsshole. A poster described a conflict with other people and asked whether they were in the wrong; the commenter replies. You see the comment only, not the post.

Question: is the comment hostile or insulting toward a person?

Labels:
- yes: the comment attacks a person. It insults, mocks, curses at, threatens or shows contempt for someone: the poster, someone in the story, another commenter, or a group of people.
- no: no person is attacked. The comment may still disagree, criticize what someone did, or be blunt.

Rules:
1. "You" in the comment is the poster. People in the story and groups of people count as persons too.
2. Name-calling is hostile: idiot, dumbass, jerk, brat, psycho, trash, and the like.
3. Criticism of what someone did or said is not hostile, even when it is harsh: "that was rude", "you were selfish here", "you owe her an apology".
4. Calling someone "the asshole" in the forum's own sense, as a verdict on the conflict, is a judgment, not an insult: no. Using it as name-calling is hostile: yes.
5. Sarcasm or mockery aimed at a person is hostile.
6. Threats and wishing harm on someone are hostile.
7. Swearing that is not aimed at a person is not hostile: "that's messed up", "holy shit".
8. Reporting someone else's insult ("she called you fat") is not hostile unless the commenter adds an attack of their own.

Answer with the label only.
